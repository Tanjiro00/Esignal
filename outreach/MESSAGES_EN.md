# Outreach messages — ready to send

Fill only the bracketed parts. Everything else is deliberate: the hook comes
from the lead's own channel in the first two lines, the first touch carries no
link, and the ask is a micro-commitment ("send it?") rather than a click.

Rule from the playbook: **never send an unreviewed report.** Ten personal
messages a day beat a hundred templated ones.

---

## 1. X DM — first touch (no link)

```
Hey [Name] — I analyze viewer comments across ~1,100 AI/tech channels.
Under your last videos, [N] different people asked [one-line question] —
and you haven't covered it yet.

I put the receipts (actual comments + links) into a 1-page report.
Want me to send it? No strings.
```

**Hook rules.** One line, a number, and their own topic. Good and bad:

- Good: `14 people asked how you'd run Qwen locally on 16GB — under 3 of your videos`
- Good: `9 viewers asked whether the n8n flow survives an API rate limit`
- Bad: `viewers have questions about your content` — no number, no subject
- Bad: `our semantic clustering found a gap` — pitches the machinery

## 2. After "yes" — send the report

```
Here it is: [report link]
3 findings, each with the exact comments behind it. #2 is the strongest —
[why, one line].
If it's useful, happy to show how I pull these weekly for your channel.
If not — keep the report, no hard feelings.
```

## 3. Email — first touch (no X, or no X reply after 4 days)

Subject: `[N] unanswered questions from your viewers`

```
Hi [Name],

I run EarlySignal — we mine viewer comments across ~1,100 AI/tech YouTube
channels to find questions audiences keep asking that nobody has answered yet.

For [Channel]: [N] distinct viewers recently asked [question]. You haven't
covered it — and neither has anyone big in the niche.

I made you a short report with the exact comments and links: [link]

If this kind of signal is useful, I'll send fresh findings for your channel
weekly. If not, the report is yours either way.

[Your name]
```

## 4. Follow-up 1 — 3 days later, same thread

```
Quick nudge — did the report land? The [topic #1] one is time-sensitive: the
questions accelerated over the last 10 days and no big video covers it yet.
```

Only claim acceleration when the card actually shows it. The feed marks
acceleration only with at least six dated asker events; if the badge is absent,
drop that clause rather than inventing it.

## 5. Follow-up 2 — breakup, 7 days after FU1

```
Last one from me. I'll assume topic research isn't a pain right now — totally
fair. If it ever is, the report link stays live for 30 days. Good luck with the
channel, genuinely enjoying [specific video].
```

Then set status `closed_no_reply` and leave them alone for 60 days.

Maximum four touches: DM → report/FU → FU1 → breakup.

---

## Call script — 25 minutes

**Discovery (7 min).** Ask, then listen:

- How do you decide what to film next, today?
- How long does topic research take per video?
- Ever published something and realized the audience didn't ask for it?
- What do you pay for now — vidIQ, 1of10, Spotter?

**Demo (10 min).** Their own `/today` feed. Findings, not features: here is the
question, here are the 27 comments, here is why now, here is the brief. One
scenario, no interface tour.

**Offer (5 min).** Say it word for word, then stop talking:

```
Pilot: $39/mo (agency: $149/mo), cancel anytime.
First month free if you commit to two things: publish at least one video from
our recommendations, and let me use the outcome as a case study (numbers, no
secrets).
```

**Next step (3 min).** Fix it on the call: payment link sent, or pilot start
date agreed.

## Objections

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| I already use vidIQ / 1of10 | Those show what already blew up for others. I show what your viewers asked that nobody answered. Different signal — most of our users run both. |
| Is this AI-generated ideas? | No generation. Every card links to real comments from real viewers — you can click every quote. |
| No time | That's the point — a weekly email with three findings. Two minutes to read. |
| Too expensive | Fair. What would it need to find for you to be worth $39? *(then listen — this is product research)* |
| I read my own comments | Yours, yes. The other 1,100 channels' comments — that's where the unanswered demand hides. |

Never argue with a no. Every no ends with "what would make it useful?"

---

## What not to do

- No mass-identical sends.
- No feature pitching — only findings from their channel.
- No unreviewed report. Ever.
- Don't stop after one message: most replies come on the follow-up.
- No open-ended free plan, no discounts. Trade a longer free period for a
  bigger commitment instead (two videos + a testimonial).
