# Product boundary

The Signals product loop supports both live evidence and clearly labeled
deterministic demo data:

```text
open signal → inspect evidence → save/dismiss → create brief
→ link published outcome
```

Slices 2–7 additionally run real YouTube discovery, official metadata
enrichment, historical snapshots, channel baselines, and video-level velocity
and outlier features. A versioned local embedding and hybrid entity/semantic
clustering pipeline groups eligible videos into confirmed topics, appends topic
snapshots, calculates transparent score components and lifecycle stages, and
publishes real signals into the same product loop.

Promising topic members receive top and newest comment samples. Deterministic
classification maps comments to the demand taxonomy, filters praise/spam, and
forms customer-visible clusters only after the same broad intent appears across
multiple commenters, videos, and channels. Cluster score, date range, sampling
limitation, verbatim snippets, and source-video links remain visible in signal
detail.

For selected high-value videos, public captions add extractive summaries,
entities, narrative format, and short timestamped evidence excerpts. Transcript
context enriches topic embeddings without becoming a required signal input:
videos with no captions continue through the full product loop.

The feed automatically opens live data when live signals exist and offers an
explicit Demo dataset switch. A live topic needs evidence from at least two
independent channels. Audience demand contributes its specified 15% positive
weight only when a confirmed stored cluster exists; channel fit remains visibly
provisional until Slice 9.
